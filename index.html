What the numbers mean
Now tab
Hourly snapshots of each video's public counters. Views per hour and last-24-hour figures are differences between snapshots. Subscriber counts are rounded by YouTube.
Long / Shorts tabs
YouTube Analytics, split by YouTube's own label for each view. It is about three days behind.
Stayed
Engaged views ÷ views: the share of Shorts views that didn't swipe away at the start.
Avg % viewed
Average share of the video watched per view. Shorts pass 100% when viewers loop.
Hook at 0:30
Share still watching 30 seconds in; the median across the channel's long videos with a retention curve.
Top Short's share
The most-viewed Short's share of the channel's Shorts views in 90 days.
Browse
YouTube's "Browse features" source: the home page and the subscriptions feed. Most of it is people who aren't subscribed.
Non-subscribers
Share of a video's 90-day views from people not subscribed to the channel.
First 2 / 7 days
Views on the upload's first two or seven YouTube days (Pacific time), from Analytics.
Not available
Impressions and click-through rate exist only in YouTube Studio.
